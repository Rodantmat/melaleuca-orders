What’s Included in This Bundle

Keep teeth clean and healthy with your choice of three tooth polishes or tooth gels that come in fluoride and fluoride-free options.

  • YOUR CHOICE OF EXCEED TOTAL CARE TOOTH POLISH WITH FLUORIDE ❯

    Exceed Total Care Tooth Polishes feature ProRadiant Technology® and fluoride to fight plaque and tartar buildup, freshen breath, and whiten teeth without damaging tooth enamel. Available in Peppermint and Spearmint flavors.

  • YOUR CHOICE OF EXCEED ESSENTIAL OIL TOOTH POLISH ❯

    Exceed Essential Oil Tooth Polishes are fluoride-free and use papain (naturally sourced from papaya), baking soda, and Pure Essential Oils. Only Exceed Tooth Polishes feature ProRadiant Technology to deeply clean and whiten teeth without damaging tooth enamel, giving you a radiant smile! Available in Cinnamon and Fresh Mint flavors.

  • YOUR CHOICE OF KOALA PALS TOOTH GELS ❯

    Deliciously flavored fluoride and fluoride-free tooth gels, powered by baking soda and naturally sweetened with xylitol.

  • CLASSIC TOOTH POLISH: COOL MINT WITH FLUORIDE ❯

    Classic Tooth Polish combines papain, T40-C3 Melaleuca Oil, xylitol, and myrrh to give you a fresher, whiter smile. It also features fluoride to help fight against cavities.

  • CLASSIC TOOTH POLISH: FRESH MINT FLUORIDE-FREE ❯

    Classic Tooth Polish combines papain, T40-C3 Melaleuca Oil, xylitol, and myrrh to give you a fresher, whiter smile.

Your Gateway to Health

The mouth is the gateway to your body’s health and a common entry point for germs and bacteria. There is a strong connection between your oral health and your overall health, including your immune system!

Uncontrolled oral bacteria can lead to periodontal disease and tooth decay. As these conditions worsen, bacteria can move from your mouth to the rest of your body.

The best way to limit bacteria in the mouth is by brushing and flossing regularly!
