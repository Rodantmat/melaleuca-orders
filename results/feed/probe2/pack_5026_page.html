About

CLEAN DISHES,

CLEAN STREAMS, CLEAN CONSCIENCE

FOR: Sparkling clean dishes

  • Cuts grease and breaks down food quickly and thoroughly
  • Concentrated to be powerful and effective. One bottle cleans over 500 dishes!
  • Powered by gentle, plant-derived ingredients

Replaces: Hand dishwashing liquids like Dawn, Palmolive, and Gain

How to Use It

  • Step 1

    ​Save your drain. Discard any food waste.

  • Step 2

    ​Fill sink with warm water.

  • Step 3

    Add 1 squirt of Lemon Brite.​

  • Step 4

    Let soiled dishes soak for up to 10 minutes. Scrub as needed.​

  • Step 5

    ​Rinse!

The goal of EcoSense® is to help you clean your home without compromising your family’s safety, the environment, or your wallet.

​You shouldn’t have to choose between sparkling clean dishes and your family’s wellness. That’s why Lemon Brite is formulated with safer ingredients that clean dishes just as well as brands that use harsh chemicals and it doesn’t cost an arm and a leg!

Dishwashing is about more than just cleaning your dishes. It’s also about making them safe for food contact. After all, whatever is on our dishes can also go into our bodies.

​​Lemon Brite’s naturally derived ingredients are tough on grease and food, but gentle on your hands. Using Lemon Brite gives you peace of mind knowing you are taking care of your family and the environment while still getting your dishes cleaner than ever before.

You can always use a little extra help cleaning the dishes. So, while you get suds up to your elbows, Lemon Brite is also hard at work.​

​​Lemon Brite begins working on your dishes the moment they hit the suds. It easily cuts grease and breaks down food quickly and thoroughly with naturally derived, biodegradable cleaning agents like Citric Acid.

Lemon Brite is formulated with 99% biodegradable ingredients, and is ultra-concentrated which means you can wash A LOT of dishes with one small bottle. One bottle of Lemon Brite can wash over 500 dishes!​

Lemon Brite is just one more way you contribute to a brighter tomorrow.

Effective dish-cleaning power in an eco-friendly, safer-for-your-home formula — Lemon Brite is truly an unbeatable value! ​Lemon Brite’s ultra-concentrated formula saves you money, sinkful after sinkful.​

A Fresh Dishwashing Indulgence.

What’s better than spending less time scrubbing at the sink? Spending those brief moments enjoying a fresh fragrance. ​With three amazing fragrances, washing dishes will never feel dull thanks to Lemon Brite.​

  • Tropical Lemon Brite adds a refreshing element of the islands to your day with notes of exotic flowers and fruit.​

  • Original Lemon Brite energizes your senses with a fresh hit of citrus.​

  • Green Apple Lemon Brite has a recharging punch of fruity spice.​

Let Us Help with the Dishes

EcoSense Dish Care delivers cleaner dishes with less work.

  • Lemon Brite Hand Dishwashing Liquid

    Powered by biodegradable ingredients, this dish soap quickly breaks down food to save you scrubbing time at the sink.

  • Diamond Brite Automatic Dishwasher Detergent

    Diamond Brite uses natural enzymes to effectively break down food and remove stains, giving your dishes a spot-free shine.

  • EcoSense Rinse Aid

    EcoSense Rinse Aid relies on naturally derived ingredients to deliver spotless, sparkling dishes after every wash, and it even helps your dishes dry better.

Safer, effective, eco-friendly, affordable cleaning products.
