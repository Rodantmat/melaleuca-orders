About

Mix, Match, and Save 20%!

Finding the right underarm care shouldn’t be one-size-fits-all. This customizable 3-pack gives you the freedom to choose between women and men’s antiperspirant and/or deodorant in a variety of scents and formulas: Alloy, Affinia, or Melaleuca Herbal.

Each one is designed to deliver reliable freshness and comfort you can count on throughout the day. Whether you prefer crisp and clean, soft and refined, or naturally fresh, this bundle makes it easy to stock up on favorites or explore new options!

What's Included in This Bundle:

Mix and match your favorites from Alloy, Affinia, or Melaleuca Herbal.

  • YOUR CHOICE OF AFFINIA ANTIPERSPIRANT & DEODORANT

    Stay dry and smell fresh—it’s no sweat. This antiperspirant deodorant neutralizes odors and absorbs moisture for up to 48 hours!

  • ALLOY ANTIPERSPIRANT & DEODORANT

    Get 48 hours of wetness protection! Using the latest in scientific skin care advancement, we updated Alloy Antiperspirant & Deodorant to a clean, nonirritating formula that protects and conditions.

  • YOUR CHOICE OF AFFINIA DEODORANT

    This deodorant helps absorb wetness and neutralize odor at the source, keeping you feeling dry and fresh for 48 hours!

  • ALLOY DEODORANT

    Keep odors away and excess moisture at bay—for a full 48 hours! Engineered with a fresh, masculine scent (your shirts and senses will thank you).

  • MELALEUCA HERBAL DEODORANT

    An herbal approach to 24-hour odor protection that is gentle, strong, and lasts all day. Ingredients like zinc ricinoleate, zea mays starch, and baking soda work together to both deodorize and absorb odors.
